PITN — Persistent Industrial Identity for the ROBOPARTS Platform

Industrial Identity Infrastructure & Operating Model

PITN (Persistent Industrial Identity) provides the persistent identity layer for ROBOPARTS, connecting manufacturer part numbers, distributor SKUs, OEM references, BOM references, asset identifiers, serial numbers, legacy identifiers, and other recognized industrial references to persistent industrial entities where their relationships have been established.
